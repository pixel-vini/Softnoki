Soft Noki privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What we collect

Soft Noki does not collect, send or sell your personal data. The name you enter on the first screen and every note you write are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see them.

Accounts, ads and tracking

The app has no account system, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts (Fredoka and Quicksand) from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when the fonts are requested, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a note with the Delete button inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

Children

Soft Noki does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
